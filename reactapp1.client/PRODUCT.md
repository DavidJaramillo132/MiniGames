# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Casual and competitive multiplayer gamers looking for instant, browser-based, high-stakes minigames (Tic-Tac-Toe, Memory, Trivia Quiz) with live rival matching, ELO climbing, and personal stats.

## Product Purpose

PlayHub provides immediate real-time multiplayer minigames with zero download friction, pairing live opponents in tactical duel arenas with competitive leaderboards and instant feedback.

## Positioning

The cyber-arcade multiplayer platform: lightning-fast real-time matchmaking powered by SignalR, rich arcade audiovisual feedback, tactile competitive gameplay, and zero fluff.

## Operating Context

Desktop and mobile browsers, fast gaming sessions between tasks or dedicated competitive sessions, dark ambient environments where high-contrast tactical visuals and responsive feedback dominate.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities: Real-time SignalR matchmaking and duel gameplay, room creation and discovery, global and game-specific leaderboards, persistent JWT authentication, English and Spanish localization (i18n), Web Audio synthesized sound fx.
- Constraints: Pure visual and aesthetic redesign. Technical logic, SignalR hub contracts, routing, authentication, and translation keys must remain 100% intact and functional.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: PlayHub
- Visual World: Arcade Cibernético Premium (Sophisticated dark canvas, sharp electric cyan `#00f0ff`, laser amber `#ffb703`, and neon emerald `#00f5a0` telemetry, tactile hardware-inspired containers, precision typography).
- Tone: Competitive, energetic, confident, crisp, and high-performance. Avoid cartoonish, childish, or generic AI-slop tropes.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing functional React 19 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind CSS + SignalR application.
- Web Audio API synthesizer for tactile game tones.
- Brand logo asset `/logo.png`.

## Product Principles

1. **Instant Action**: Every surface prioritizes jumping straight into a match with clear, unhesitating calls to action.
2. **Tactile Arcade Craft**: Interactive components feel physical, responsive, and tactile with crisp feedback on hover, focus, and press.
3. **Telemetry & Legibility**: Data, scores, timers, and online player counts are legible at a glance with strict WCAG AA contrast compliance.
4. **Cohesive Worldbuilding**: Every screen, from landing to lobby to match duel, belongs to the exact same cyber-arcade universe.
